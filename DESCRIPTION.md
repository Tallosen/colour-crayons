# Материалы для подачи

## Название
Colour Crayons

## Короткое описание (до 80 знаков)
Tap a crayon, paint a whole row, redraw the picture.

## Описание для витрины
Colour Crayons is a calm little puzzle about drawing.

Each crayon paints an entire row or column at once, and every new colour
covers the one before it. Your job is to find the right order of strokes
and reproduce the picture at the top.

Simple to pick up, harder than it looks. No timers, no ads inside the game,
no accounts — just you, a handful of crayons and a warm paper page.

- 3×3 to 5×5 grids
- endless hand-made-looking pictures
- three stars for a clean solution
- one tap per move, plays with one hand
- 15 languages
