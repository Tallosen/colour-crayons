Privacy Policy

Colour Crayons — Vacky Games · Last updated: 5 September 2026

Colour Crayons is a puzzle game made by Vacky Games. This policy explains what the game does and does not do with your data. In short: it collects nothing.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for, collect, store or transmit any personal information. There is no sign-up, no account, no email, no location and no contact data.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress — the current picture number, earned stars, chosen language and the sound setting — in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device, is not readable by us, and is removed when you clear your browser data.

Analytics and advertising

The game itself contains no analytics, no tracking pixels and no advertising code. When played inside a host platform such as YouTube Playables, that platform may show ads and collect data under its own privacy policy, which we do not control.

Third parties

The game makes no network requests. All files are bundled locally, so nothing is loaded from external servers while you play.

Children

Colour Crayons is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no data at all, we collect no data from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
